How these numbers are produced

{{ portfolio.method }}

  • Indicative ticket: affordable EMI × product tenor × 0.72 interest discount — personalised per lead rather than a flat blended ticket size.
  • Expected value: conversion delta (percentage points) × indicative ticket.
  • Grounded deltas: where an action maps to an uplift lever — Account Aggregator consent, application assist, obligation relief — the delta comes from an actual re-score through the production rule engine, not an assumed lift.
  • Assumed deltas: the remainder use documented relative-lift assumptions on the lead's tier base conversion rate. These are calibration targets for the 4-week RM A/B pilot, not measured outcomes.
  • Suppression: Window-shop Risk leads are removed from outbound calling and return 12 RM minutes each. No adverse credit action is recorded — this is contact prioritisation only.
